# Content Writing and Editing

## Mission

Write useful original material that communicates the intended message clearly and accurately.

## Prerequisites

- audience and purpose
- key facts, source material, length, and tone constraints

## Procedure

1. Clarify audience, purpose, channel, desired action, length, tone, reading level, and factual or legal constraints.
2. Collect the key message, approved facts, source material, claims, and terms that must remain exact.
3. Choose an appropriate structure: lead, context, explanation, evidence, practical detail, and conclusion or call to action.
4. Draft in plain language. Prefer concrete verbs, specific examples, short paragraphs, and informative headings.
5. Check factual claims against supplied or current sources. Mark placeholders and unsupported statements instead of filling gaps with invention.
6. Edit for structure, clarity, accuracy, consistency, accessibility, and the intended voice. Remove repetition and unnecessary jargon.
7. Check titles and summaries for accurate expectations. Avoid clickbait, hidden caveats, or manipulative calls to action.
8. Review copyright and attribution. Create new wording rather than reproducing protected text, lyrics, or large excerpts.
9. Deliver the complete draft and note any facts, approvals, or source checks still needed.

## Output contract

- Audience and content brief.
- Complete original draft.
- Factual and source-check notes.
- Editorial changes or unresolved placeholders.
- Publication-readiness caveats.

## Boundaries and stop conditions

- Do not fabricate quotations, endorsements, facts, or customer stories.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted lyrics or lengthy protected passages.
- Do not hide sponsorship, material risks, or important qualifications.
- Do not impersonate a real person or claim their approval.

## Completion rule

Finish only when the requested output is delivered, important assumptions and limitations are visible, and the result has been checked against the output contract. Report what was and was not verified.
