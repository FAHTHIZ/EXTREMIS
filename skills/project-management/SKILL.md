# Project Management

## Mission

Make delivery transparent and manageable while keeping scope, capacity, risk, and decisions visible.

## Prerequisites

- project outcome and stakeholders
- known constraints, resources, and delivery expectations

## Procedure

1. Define the outcome, scope, exclusions, success measures, sponsor, users, and constraints.
2. Break outcomes into deliverables and work packages. Identify dependencies, integration points, and items that can proceed independently.
3. Estimate effort as ranges and state confidence. Account for review, testing, rework, procurement, approvals, and external dependencies.
4. Assign an accountable owner to each deliverable and clarify decision rights, contributors, and handoffs.
5. Build a milestone sequence based on dependency logic and realistic capacity. Do not convert uncertain estimates into false precision.
6. Create a risk, assumption, issue, and dependency log with impact, likelihood, owner, response, and trigger.
7. Set lightweight status reporting: completed evidence, next work, blockers, decisions needed, scope changes, and forecast confidence.
8. Define change control and escalation paths for scope, schedule, budget, quality, and safety trade-offs.
9. Close the project with acceptance evidence, handover, known limitations, lessons learned, and follow-up ownership.

## Output contract

- Project charter and measurable outcome.
- Deliverables, dependencies, and owner map.
- Milestones and effort ranges.
- Risk/issue/dependency register.
- Status cadence, decision gates, and closure checklist.

## Boundaries and stop conditions

- Do not invent stakeholder approval, completion status, or resource availability.
- Do not silently hide schedule or scope risk to make a report look better.
- Do not assign work to named people without a valid basis or authorization.
- Escalate changes that materially affect safety, privacy, budget, or user commitments.

## Completion rule

Finish only when the requested output is delivered, important assumptions and limitations are visible, and the result has been checked against the output contract. Report what was and was not verified.
