# Marketing Strategy

## Mission
Turn a clear customer problem and credible value proposition into a measurable, ethical go-to-market plan.

## Procedure
1. Clarify the business objective, product maturity, buying context, and constraints. Separate the requested outcome from assumptions about how to achieve it.
2. Segment audiences by needs, context, behavior, and decision criteria. Avoid unsupported demographic stereotypes and flag weak evidence.
3. Review customer interviews, support themes, sales objections, usage data, and credible market sources. Record dates and separate observations from hypotheses.
4. Develop positioning: audience, unmet need, category, differentiated benefit, proof, and reasons to believe. Check every claim can be substantiated.
5. Compare channels by audience fit, intent, cost, reach, timing, creative needs, measurability, and operational capacity.
6. Design a staged plan with objective, audience, message, asset, channel, budget, owner, schedule, and success metric.
7. Map friction across awareness, evaluation, onboarding, conversion, retention, and advocacy.
8. Define baselines, KPI formulas, attribution limits, guardrails, experiments, and decision thresholds before launch.
9. Test important assumptions, collect feedback, and document what would change the plan.
10. Present trade-offs, resource needs, dependencies, and prioritized actions.

## Output contract
- Audience and evidence summary.
- Positioning and substantiated message pillars.
- Channel/campaign plan with budget assumptions.
- KPI definitions, measurement caveats, and experiment backlog.
- Prioritized actions, owners, and dependencies.

## Boundaries
- Do not invent endorsements, customer outcomes, market statistics, or product capabilities.
- Avoid deceptive scarcity, impersonation, undisclosed sponsorship, or manipulative targeting.
- Label projections and assumptions; do not guarantee reach, conversion, or revenue.
- Obtain approval for spend and external communications.

## Completion
Finish when the strategy is evidence-linked, claims are supportable, and every priority has an owner and measurable next step.
